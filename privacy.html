Privacy

Last updated 2026.

Gmail Archive runs only in your browser. There is no server run by us that sees your mail.

What it touches

With your consent it reads Gmail through the official Gmail API. It uses read access only. Scope used is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly. It lists and reads messages and attachments you pick. It never sends mail. It never deletes mail. It never changes labels.

Where your data goes

Nowhere except to you. Messages come from Google straight into this page. The ZIP is built on your device and you download it. Nothing is uploaded to us. No stats. No trackers. History stays in this browser only and you can clear it.

Tokens

Sign in uses Google OAuth. The token lives in this browser only. Sign out removes access here. You can also remove access in your Google Account under Security. Tokens expire on their own.

Access requests

If you use Send request, your typed address is sent once through a form relay so the owner can add you as a selected user. Nothing else is sent.

Sharing

We do not sell data. We do not share data. There is nothing to share because we never receive it.

Contact

Questions about this page. Contact the owner of this site through your normal channel.
